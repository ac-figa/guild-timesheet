        Who worked what, by day

        For each day, add a project and the crew members who worked on it — this is just a plan of who was where, separate from their actual hours. When you later enter someone’s hours for a day they’re recorded here, the project fills in automatically. If someone worked more than one project in a day, add them under each one. Entering hours against a different project than what’s recorded here won’t be blocked — you’ll just see a small note about it.

        Retrofit project

        Crew who actually did the traffic-light work log their hours directly under this project in the Enter Hours tab, just like any other project. Then, for each day below, enter how many traffic lights were done — that sets an hours budget (traffic lights × hours per light). Whatever budget is left over after the hours already logged directly can be given to other crew members here, pulled out of whatever other projects they logged hours on this week, so their weekly total doesn’t change — only which project the hours are billed to.
